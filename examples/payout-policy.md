# Bounty Payout Policy

Validators should recommend a payout only when:

1. The claim maps to the bounty scope.
2. The pull request or commit plausibly fixes the reported issue.
3. The reproduction notes describe how the issue was verified.
4. The evidence is readable from public URLs.
5. The requested amount is within the reward band.

If evidence is missing, unreadable, duplicated, or outside scope, the verdict
should be `needs_review` or `reject`.
